Quick Answers

Player help.

Try these steps first. If the issue continues, send us a report and we’ll take a closer look.

The game is not starting or has stopped responding.

Close and reopen the game, restart your device, and confirm that both your operating system and Football Maestro Mini are up to date. Make sure your device also has enough free storage. If the problem continues, contact us with your device and game-version details.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Football Maestro Mini supports offline play. Some store, update, advertising, or support functions may still require an internet connection.

My progress looks wrong or is missing.

Do not uninstall the game or clear its app data while progress is missing, as this may remove information useful for recovery. Send us a support request with your device, game version, player or support ID, and a description of when the issue began.

I have a question about a purchase.

Check the purchase history for the Google Play or App Store account used at checkout. If an item is missing or a charge looks incorrect, send us the store receipt or order number through the support form. Do not include passwords or full payment-card details.

Where can I read the Privacy Policy and Terms?

You can review the current Privacy Policy and Terms of Service on our website.

Still Need Help?

Talk to the team.

Use our support form to report a bug, ask a question, or tell us where you need help. The form opens securely in Google Forms.

Please avoid sharing passwords, full payment-card numbers, or other sensitive personal information.
